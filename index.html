Gaby Striano graduated from Northwestern University in 2025 with a B.S. in journalism. Her work has appeared in ABC News and Retro Report.

Gaby has covered all aspects of pop culture, including film and TV, sports and travel. Her behind-the-scenes approach to entertainment journalism allows viewers and readers to engage with their favorite fads and hobbies in new ways.

Based in — New York, NY
